01 / THE THREE OF US

A few familiar faces.

Three different paths brought us to the same campus. Here’s a little of what makes each of us, us.
01

The visual thinker

Ardiansyah

Visual communication · Year 2nd

I’m Maya, a design student who notices the lettering on every café window. I’m exploring how illustration and thoughtful typography can make everyday information feel more human. In our team, I turn messy ideas into sketches, layouts, and things we can actually point to. My notebook usually fills up before my coffee iced matcha gets cold.

WHAT I BRING
Illustration Figma Visual storytelling
AFTER CLASS

Hobby: Film photography, secondhand bookshops, and sketching people at the campus café.

02

The curious builder

Rafli Iyyad

Computer science · Year 2nd

I’m Leo, and I like figuring out how things work — then trying to build my own version. I study computer science with a growing interest in accessible web experiences and creative coding. For this project, I connect the dots between our designs and the browser, test the small details, and ask a probably unreasonable number of questions.

WHAT I BRING
HTML & CSS JavaScript Problem-solving
AFTER CLASS

Hobby: Bouldering, making overly specific playlists, and drinking fresh H2O.

03

The story collector

Nisa

Sociology · Year 2nd

I’m Nora, a sociology student interested in the little rituals that make a place feel like home. I love listening to people and finding the story behind an ordinary moment. On our team, I help shape interview questions, make sense of our notes, and write copy inspired by The Sociological Imagination.

WHAT I BRING
Interviewing Research Writing
AFTER CLASS

Hobby: Campus radio, long walks without a destination, and weekend baking experiments.
